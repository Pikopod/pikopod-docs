## Test fixtures

Fixtures under `testdata/parity/importer/specs/` are license-checked in CI. Every file needs an entry in that directory's `SOURCES.md`, and an unlisted spec fails the build. No provider content lands without an explicit grant; "publicly fetchable" is not a grant. When a test needs a real-world shape that cannot be licensed, a synthetic fixture is generated and named `synthetic-*`.

## Your data

There is no telemetry, there are no accounts, and there is no license check. Everything pikopod persists lives in your `data_dir`. See [What leaves the machine](/operations/security#what-leaves-the-machine).
